Thailand’s power market has re-accelerated in 2026.

Electricity consumption was up 6.0% YoY in Jan–May after a 2.8% decline in 2025, while the EGAT-system peak reached a new high in April. Policy direction is simultaneously shifting toward a cleaner and more open system through PDP2026, Direct PPA, UGT and grid-flexibility measures.

Source policy: All quantitative figures use publicly available sources. Comparisons marked as changes are calculated only from published source values; no model-estimated or illustrative market figures are used.

How Thailand’s power plan is changing

Current approved target → common draft build through 2037 → longer-term 2050 range.
PDP in plain English: Thailand’s Power Development Plan sets how much electricity capacity the system needs and which technologies should supply it. AEDP2018 is today’s approved renewable benchmark; PDP2026 is the draft next plan under consultation, extending the horizon to 2050.

Thailand Power 101

Static reference • update only when market structure / rules change

Quick reference for Thailand’s power-market structure and terminology.

Who does what?

Generators

EGAT • IPP • SPP • VSPP • imports • self-generation

→

System / bulk power

EGAT plays the central role in transmission, system operation and bulk procurement under the current structure.

→

Distribution / customers

MEA serves Bangkok / metro area; PEA serves the rest of the country. New green-tariff and TPA mechanisms add procurement options.

Market-design watch: Direct PPA does not mean a fully liberalized retail market. The pilot uses third-party access to state-owned networks under defined eligibility and tariff rules.

Key concepts

PDP

Power Development Plan — long-term generation / system planning framework.

AEDP

Alternative Energy Development Plan — renewable / alternative energy targets and policy direction.

IPP

Independent Power Producer — typically large private generators selling into the system.

SPP / VSPP

Small / Very Small Power Producers, including many renewable and cogeneration assets.

Ft

Fuel adjustment component added to the base electricity tariff, adjusted periodically.

UGT

Utility Green Tariff — utility-provided green electricity product / mechanism.

Direct PPA

Direct renewable purchase between eligible buyer and generator through the network under the pilot framework.

TPA / wheeling

Third-party access to electricity networks and associated network / system charges.

BESS

Battery Energy Storage System — increasingly important for flexibility and renewable integration.

Definitions & conventions

Capacity basis matters

Utility pipeline figures use contracted-sale MW; self-use/direct-customer figures use installed MW. They are shown separately and are not summed unless a scope-consistent conversion is explicitly defined.

Data freshness

This view refreshes weekly, while official datasets update on different schedules. Each value therefore shows the publication period of the underlying datapoint.

Figure provenance: Every quantitative figure in this dashboard is tied to a public source URL. Derived comparisons (for example MoM or YoY changes) are calculated only from published observations and are identified in the embedded provenance manifest. Draft PDP scenario figures are explicitly tagged as public consultation coverage where an official machine-readable table is not available.
